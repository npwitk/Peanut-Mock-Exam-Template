\section*{Part I: True or False}
\textit{Circle \textbf{T} for True or \textbf{F} for False.}

\vspace{0.4cm}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{1.\arabic*}, left=0pt, itemsep=0.35cm]

\item \tf
A factual or conceptual statement about the course material.
It must be unambiguous and decidable from the lectures alone.



\item \tf
A statement may contain inline mathematics such as $f(x)$ or $x_t$,
or reference a standard definition or property.



\item \tf
The wording is precise. Avoid opinion-based or vague statements.
Negations such as ``not'' may be used, but only deliberately.



\item \tf
A statement may target a common misconception --- the kind of claim
that sounds right but fails on one specific detail.



\end{enumerate}
