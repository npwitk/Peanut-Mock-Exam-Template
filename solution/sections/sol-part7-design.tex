\section*{Part VII: System Design --- Solution}

\begin{keypoint}[How this part is graded]
There is no single correct architecture. Full credit requires that every
requirement in the background box is addressed by a named technology or
mechanism from this course, and that each choice is justified against its
alternative.
\end{keypoint}

\textbf{(a) First Layer of the Design}
\begin{ans}
\textbf{Reference design:} name the three components and their configuration.

\textbf{Justification:} tie each component to the quantified requirement it
satisfies.

\textbf{Guarantee and trade-off:} state the guarantee chosen and what it costs
(latency, throughput, storage, complexity).

\textbf{Also acceptable:} the alternative design and the condition under which
it would be preferred.
\end{ans}

\textbf{(b) Second Layer of the Design}
\begin{ans}
Model answer, including the data flow the question asked to draw:
source $\rightarrow$ transform $\rightarrow$ sink, with the property enforced
at each hop.
\end{ans}

\textbf{(c) Third Layer of the Design}
\begin{ans}
Model answer for the isolation / scheduling requirement, including the
behaviour under contention.
\end{ans}

\textbf{(d) Failure and Trade-offs}
\begin{ans}
\textbf{Blast radius:} what is lost and for how long.

\textbf{Recovery path:} the concrete sequence that restores service.

\textbf{What to relax on half the budget:} name the requirement, and say why
it is the cheapest one to give up.
\end{ans}

\begin{caution}
Listing technologies without justifying them scores near zero on this part.
The marks are in the \textit{because}.
\end{caution}
